# Brand Guidelines

## Palette
Anchor in deep indigo and slate tones, accented with warm amber and terracotta. Avoid oversaturated primaries or garish artificial shades.

## Light
Warm raking golden-hour light casting long, soft directional shadows. Single primary key light that illuminates natural textures.

## Composition
Off-center hero subject positioned on the thirds with generous negative space and shallow depth of field. Clean, balanced framing.

## Subject
One authentic, realistic photographic subject. Authentic and handcrafted materials with rich physical presence and tactile quality.

## Never
No neon or fluorescent cyan/magenta. No watermarks, corporate logos, text overlays, harsh ring lights, or glossy 3D renders.
